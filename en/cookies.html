Information pursuant to Art. 13 GDPR, Art. 122 of the Italian Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree No. 196/2003 as amended) and the Italian data protection authority’s guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021 (Linee guida cookie e altri strumenti di tracciamento).

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit and reads again on later visits. Similar techniques, such as the browser’s local storage, pixels or fingerprinting, are treated the same way legally and are covered by this policy.

There are:

  • Technical cookies (cookie tecnici): necessary for a website to work. They may be set without consent; providing information is sufficient.
  • Analytics cookies (cookie analitici): for statistical analysis of usage. Without consent only permitted if anonymised and treated as equivalent to technical cookies.
  • Profiling and marketing cookies (cookie di profilazione): for creating user profiles and for advertising. They may only be set with prior explicit consent.

Normally none. The website leclou.bz sets neither its own cookies nor third-party cookies and does not use comparable techniques such as local storage, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. The only exception is our hosting provider’s security protection (see below).
