The method

What is Hypoxi?

Hypoxi is a special training concept for targeted body shaping, right at the typical problem areas: stomach, hips, bottom and thighs.

Many people know the feeling: despite exercise and a mindful diet, these areas stay stubborn. This is where Hypoxi comes in, combining alternating pressure, moderate exercise and a balanced diet.

You don’t need peak athletic performance or a crash diet. The training is gentle, easy on the joints and pleasant.

How it works

The 3-pillar principle

Hypoxi
01

Alternating pressure

In special devices, such as an ergometer in a low-pressure chamber or a pressure suit on the bed, low and high pressure alternate. This is intended to stimulate circulation in problem areas that are often poorly supplied with blood and to help transport fatty acids away.

02

Moderate exercise

Light, low-effort endurance training, such as cycling while seated or lying down, gets your metabolism going. Building muscle is not the focus.

03

Balanced diet

A healthy, balanced diet supports the process. Strict diets or starvation diets are explicitly not part of it.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to be fit?

No. The exercise in Hypoxi is moderate and easy on the joints. It’s nothing like a strenuous gym workout.

Do I have to go on a diet?

No. Hypoxi deliberately doesn’t rely on strict diets but on a balanced diet. We’re happy to give you a few simple tips in the initial consultation / trial session.

Is Hypoxi right for me?

We’ll find out in a personal initial consultation / trial session. If you have health concerns or pre-existing conditions, or are pregnant, please talk to your doctor first.
